Version 1.11 Update Notes - October 22, 2026

Today, we released the Version 1.11 update for Borderlands 4. This update includes updates to Story Pack 2: FL4K and the Last Resort* content to keep a smooth gameplay experience, and balance adjustments for Loveless the Hacker*, the newest Vault Hunter. We've resolved progression blockers, adjusted boss fights, and improved tracking to get you to the new objective. For Loveless, we've updated Contagion, shuriken, and Wyrm pet AI behaviors. We've also made balance changes to other Vault Hunters.

Change Notes:

Gameplay
Story Pack 2: FL4K and the Last Resort*

    Mr. Chew joins the fight when traveling with you.
    The mission will start as expected in multiplayer when it is the first time the host is playing FL4K and the Last Resort content.
    Spawning to Providence Island is only accessible to those with paid content ownership.
    Updated Objectives not progressing due to killing enemies ahead of time or reaching areas ahead of Mr. Chew.
    Updated missions to keep their current states after reloading into the map with a save/load.
    Doors will unlock and elevator buttons will be interactable when they're supposed to.
    A reported case of invincibility has been removed from Lieutenant Harmony when she goes into her second phase of combat. She also no longer dodges throwing knives, and her Blade Attack will continue to harm you when standing close to the edge. 
    Killing Lieutenant Harmony Mission Objective will complete when using C4SH's "Vampiric Touch" Card when in Windfall.
    Ducky's combat will not start until you enter the arena.
    Lieutenant Bume will spawn during Tediore Digi-Rift World Events instead of Megan.
    Drilla Killa's will to survive after reaching 1HP has been removed. He will appropriately die when you've done enough damage to him.
    Dr. Elena Smith spawns immediately after reloading the map from UVHM.
    Tediore Employees will stay hostile towards you.
    Spaced out enemies when fighting near The Keeper's Ear Fast Travel Station.
    Enemies will join combat when fighting another group on the road near FL4K's Sanctum. 
    Unstable Ordonite damage no longer scales with player level and causes you to go down from touching it.
    Corrosive explosive plants will damage you if hit too close.
    Ocean water will no longer deal damage to you.
    Substation Control door will only unlock when all Substation Switches are activated and the icon will show as complete for all players on the big map.
    All players are able to pick up the Ordonite Bomb in multiplayer during "The Rescue".
    Digi-Rift portals will disappear for all players when they're not able to be grappled into.
    You can see ECHO-4's progress when hacking The Surly Centipede Tiki Bar, and the objective will stay active if you leave the green circle.
    Camera will only shake for the person on the zipline in multiplayer.
    Added a zipline to exit the porn stash area quickly.
    Corrected some grapple points that weren't functioning and added a grapple during "The Tainted Truth".
    Your location will match the Big Map inside the shrink rift.
    Pyroclast Field will show as discovered when you're entering that area.
    Updated cinematics for better ragdoll and cloth animation and focus.

Bounty Pack 5: Amara and the Vile Shadows**

    Bosses from Amara and the Vile Shadows are available when selecting Big Encore on the Moxxi Replay Machine.
    Dive attacks from Skydrift Threshers will damage players.
    The Thresher Experiment will make use of a flying spin attack in combat.
    Defeating Reject Prototypes will unlock the second Fast Travel Station in Amara and the Vile Shadows and the large rift will continue to work. 
    Test Alpha will no longer spawn enemies from other paid content.
    Active siphon orbs will damage you if you stand too close. 
    Lava pools will cause damage if you stand in them. 
    Rifts for traveling in and out of the Phase will display the proper effects and icons and will stay visible when viewed close up.
    The Rift in the room before the boss will remain present through save/loads and when getting killed during the Test Zero fight.
    Interactive objects will show their green highlights.
    Objectives will only be visible when they're active, and the Kill the Thresher Experiment will progress as intended.
    ECHO logs will not reappear after quitting to the main menu.
    A lever has been added to both sides of the shield during The Rift Crisis mission so it can be disabled from either side.
    Prevented some instances of Clarion Wardens getting knocked out of the arena (looking at you, Harlowe players) to prevent a blocker on mission progress.

Bounty Pack 4: Murders and Acquisitions***

    More Salt Licker enemy variants will spawn in the Toxic Scowl. Firebrands, Sluggers, and Badass Psychos will now appear.
    Stank Eyes will continue fighting players during his fight, regardless of where they are.

Bounty Pack 3: A Zane To Kill For****

    Closed boss arena exit doors.

Base Game

    Fast Traveling will complete before your character loads in, allowing you to start moving immediately.
    Horace's health pools have been adjusted. The secondary health pool from the first stage of the fight has been reduced, while the second stage heath pool has been increased. This results in a shorter flying stage and longer grounded stage.
    Added a bank in the Takedown at Hadron Abyss lobby.
    Addressed some instances of the Black Market Vending Machine being filled with inconsistent gear.
    Lilith will only teleport you once after defeating Arjay and saving/quitting.
    Vault entrances only appear after picking up three Vault Keys.
    Eridium mining drones spawn on top of terrain so you can keep the objective going during "Crystal Brawl" Mission.
    Prevented the game from resuming before you're ready at the menu when connection is lost in single player. 
    The camera won't clip into your shoulder-mounted weapon when reviving a downed player.
    We've done a pass over the new content released in the previous update to identify and correct areas that were dropping items, taking you out of the map, avoiding conflict, or getting you and enemies stuck so you can keep playing across base and paid content.
    We moved around some objects that defied gravity or clipped with the scenery around them.
    Challenges that were not progressing properly have been updated.
    Updated ECHOlocation guides and waypoints on various missions.
