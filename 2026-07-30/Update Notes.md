Version 1.9 - July 30, 2026

Weekly Update Notes - July 30th, 2026

Today’s Version 1.9 update brings exciting new content with the paid DLC of Bounty Pack 4: Murders & Acquisitions and Vault Card 4*! Additionally, Pearlescent world drops now include dedicated drop sources and three new Pearlescent weapons have been added. 

Across the rest of the game, we’ve improved boss encounters, ensured dropped loot stays in reach, and polished up some gear and abilities.

New Content

Bounty Pack 4: Murders and Acquisitions*

Levaine, leader of the Electi, has been contacted by an unlikely group of new potential allies: The Flock, a commune of pacifist Rippers who gave up violent slaughter for chill vibes. They've made camp in a polluted desert canyon known as the Toxic Scowl, but now their buzz is being harshed by The Blasphemers, an aggressive group of Rippers who have taken over a nearby Order facility.

New loot unique to this new Bounty Pack includes: 

    1 new Pearlescent weapon:
        PRISM (Jakobs Sniper Rifle)
    6 new pieces of Legendary gear:
        Silver Sliver (Daedalus Pistol)
        Overconsumption (Tediore Pistol)
        Plump Bob (Ripper Shotgun)
        Hydrowerks (Torgue Shield)
        Oxidation (Vladof Repkit)
        Loiter Sploiter (Torgue Heavy Weapon Ordnance)

Vault Card 4: Desert Dreams

Bounty Pack 4: Vault Card 4 lets you earn new rewards through gameplay, with 24 cosmetic items and four pieces of rerollable gear to unlock! Here's the full rundown of the new Vault Card's unlocks:

    4 rerollable pieces of Legendary gear:
        Divided Glow (Order Assault Rifle)
        Eradication (Vladof Assault Rifle)

        Pressure Kettle (Daedalus Grenade Ordnance)
    1 Unique Head for each base game Vault Hunter

    5 Weapon Skins
    5 Vehicle Skins
    4 ECHO-4 Drone Skins
    2 ECHO-4 Attachments

Pearlescents and Dedicated Drops

The eight Pearlescent weapons from the Version 1.7 update now have dedicated drop sources, in addition to being world drops. Additionally, we have added two new Pearlescent weapons and one Legendary with dedicated spots in Kairos; you'll have to discover them for yourself to see what they do!

    Eight Pearlescents now have dedicated drop sources
        Solar Temper - Timekeeper
        Locust - Idolator Sol
        Unkempt Herald - Vault Guardian Inceptus
        Juliets Sparkle - Callis
        Abyss - Vile Lictor
        Screwstonian - Vault Guardian Origo
        Constable - Bloomreaper
        Gomie - Thol
    Two new Pearlescents and one new Legendary
        New Pearlescent Gun - Vault Guardian Radix 
        New Pearlescent Gun - Subjugator
        New Legendary Gun - Ordonite Generator Activity bosses
    To accommodate the new dedicated drop sources, we have also decreased their world drop rates.
