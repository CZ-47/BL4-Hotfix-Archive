New Vault Hunter: Loveless the Hacker*

Loveless the Hacker, an all-new Vault Hunter, is now available as paid DLC! To play as Loveless in Borderlands 4, you must own the Loveless the Hacker DLC. Loveless the Hacker is included with Borderlands 4 Super Deluxe Edition and the Borderlands 4 Vault Hunter Pack, and is also available for separate purchase. 

Two minds exist inside Loveless, forming a synchronized set of lethal skills that strike as one. Through a symbiotic melding of the biological and digital, this ex-Anshin hacker-turned-assassin has become a superior lifeform. Equipped with the highest-end cybernetics, Loveless shares her psyche with a digital virus of her own creation, which can digistruct into a physical Wyrm form to act as a deadly extension of her shared, perfection-seeking will.

Loveless' abilities are aggressive and agile, using a cybernetic arsenal to cut down enemies with Corrosive and Radiation damage. Throw her Plague Star shuriken to slice through mobs while spreading a withering Contagion, outsmart opponents with the stealthy misdirection of her Visual Paradox Nexus that can cloak Loveless and spawns a decoy in her place, or sic her Malignant Construct on targets and watch your symbiote Wyrm companion eradicate them with its tail whips and toxic breath. Loveless' Action Skills have added utility through low-cooldown power spikes called Functions and high-impact finishers called Executables.

Functions: Tap Action Skill an additional time to activate. Typically very low cooldown and fast bursts of power.

Executables: Press and hold Action Skill to activate. These have longer cooldowns with huge payoffs.

Loveless the Hacker's Action Skills:

    RAM: When Loveless inflicts a Critical Hit or applies a Status Effect she gains RAM. When RAM is filled, her Melee Attack will be Empowered, gaining increased Damage Dealt, but consuming all RAM. RAM is lost over time when none has been gained recently. The more filled RAM is, Loveless gains increased Gun Damage.

Loveless the Hacker's Action Skills:

    Plague Star (Virology skill tree): Loveless digistructs a massive Shuriken into her hand and throws it at her target, Piercing enemies, dealing Corrosive Action Skill Damage, and inflicting enemies with Contagion, which deals Corrosive Status Effect Damage over time for a duration. If an enemy suffering from Contagion dies, the virus seeks new hosts and inflicts them with a portion of the Contagion Stacks that the original enemy had. 
    Executable: Press and hold this Action Skill to detonate all Contagions, consuming them and dealing Corrosive Action Skill Damage in an area.
    Visual Paradox Nexus (Hack / Slash skill tree): Loveless activates her Visual Paradox Nexus for a duration, increasing Movement Speed and Critical Hit Damage. Killing an enemy while Visual Paradox Nexus is active causes Loveless to enter Stealth for a brief duration and creates a Clone at the location of the killed enemy. Only one Clone can exist at a time. 
        Function: Pressing Action Skill again will send out a Slash with a Kusarigama, creating a wave of Radiation Melee Damage. If Loveless is in Stealth, the Kusarigama attack will Critically Hit and end Stealth.
    Malignant Construct (Manifestation skill tree): Press Action Skill to Summon a digital Wyrm to fight by Loveless' side. When Loveless summons Wyrm, she gains Symbiosis Stacks, allowing her to benefit from Symbiosis Effects. Symbiosis Stacks deplete over time. Loveless gains Symbiosis when Wyrm kills an enemy or by using certain Passives.
        Function: Press Action Skill while Wyrm is out to order them to Teleport towards an enemy, dealing Radiation Damage in an area around them. 
        Executable: Press and hold Action Skill while Wyrm is out to order them to use a powerful Breath Attack on an enemy, dealing Radiation Melee Damage.

In a ploy to secure Providence's precious Ordonite resource, Tediore has sent an expeditionary group led by five elite managers who serve the ruthless Lieutenant Harmony. 

Venture into the verdant wilds and aid FL4K and their pet in the urgent fight against Tediore. Hunt down the managers however you see fit and show them that Vault Hunters can never be tamed.

Bounty Pack 5: Amara and the Vile Shadows**

New content in the paid DLC of Bounty Pack 5: Amara and the Vile Shadows

    New mission featuring a new boss and two new mini-bosses that can drop a new Pearlescent weapon and six new pieces of Legendary loot!
    Investigate the source of the Phase anomalies alongside Amara and battle Vile enemies warped by this unstable energy
    Complete the mission to score additional rewards: 
        New Vault Hunter Skin usable by all Vault Hunters
        New Vehicle
        New ECHO-4 Drone Skin

Kairos was forever changed by the cataclysmic arrival of the Elpis moon, and now the planet's showing signs of coming apart completely. Shards of Ordonite continue to spread corruption, and the recent appearance of unstable Phase rifts threatens to break the bonds of reality. With her Siren powers and attunement to Phase energies, Amara has pinpointed a possible source of the worsening instability: one of Vile Lictor's old laboratories, where Order Synths that once served him lie dormant.

In this all-new repeatable mission, team up with Amara to track down the Phase disturbances and stop their propagation before they unravel the known universe. Be ready for anything with these Phase rifts; there's no telling what you'll see on the other side.

Once you've acquired Bounty Pack 5: Amara and the Vile Shadows, you'll be able to start its repeatable mission after you've completed the "A Lot to Process" mission in the Fadefields during the main campaign. You'll find Bounty Pack 5's mission-starting chunk of Ordonite near any Bounty Board in the major hub areas, which will be marked on your ECHO map as a new mission.

By farming the new boss, two new mini-bosses, and new Vile enemy types in Bounty Pack 5: Amara and the Vile Shadows, you can score a new Pearlescent weapon and six new pieces of Legendary loot!

    1 new Pearlescent weapon:

    6 new pieces of Legendary gear:
        Triplicate (Vladof Assault Rifle)
        Pragmate (Daedalus Shotgun)
        Enumeration (Ripper SMG)
        Clarity (Maliwan Sniper Rifle)
        Wind Skimmer (Jakobs Sniper Rifle)

Bounty Pack 5: Vault Card 5**

While Bounty Pack 5: Amara and the Vile Shadows represents the new narrative-focused content, Bounty Pack 5: Vault Card 5 lets you earn new space-age rewards through gameplay, with 24 cosmetic items and four pieces of rerollable gear to unlock! Here's the full rundown of the new Vault Card's unlocks:

    4 rerollable pieces of Legendary gear:
        Shield Overflow (Daedalus Pistol)
        Flash Fuel (Jakobs Assault Rifle)
        Tempered Essence (Vladof SMG )
        Alignment (Order Grande Ordnance)
    4 Vault Hunter Heads usable by all Vault Hunters
    4 Vault Hunter Skins usable by all Vault Hunters
    5 Weapon Skins
    5 Vehicle Skins
    4 ECHO-4 Drone Skins
    2 ECHO-4 Attachments

Playing Borderlands 4 with a Vault Card active gives you more loot and XP via Daily and Weekly Challenges, and can be leveled up at your own pace (no time limits or expiring rewards). Once you own a Bounty Pack's Vault Card, you can activate that Vault Card via the Vault Card screen in your ECHO menu. Vault Card progression works just like leveling up: it's all driven by how much XP you're gaining. By exploring Kairos, killing enemies, and completing quests, you'll fill up your Vault Card progression in addition to your regular XP!
